What the directory links to

The directory prefers provider-owned deletion pages, privacy dashboards, export tools, security pages and support articles. A resource is labeled official only when first-party provenance is explicitly recorded.

AI accounts and AI activity are different

For products such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot, the guide separates permanent account deletion from chat history, model activity, privacy requests and parent-account controls. Destructive links that affect an entire Google or Microsoft account are marked clearly.

Before deleting an account

Export anything you want to keep, check active subscriptions, review connected apps, and confirm whether deletion has a waiting period. Some providers let you reactivate during a grace period, while others make deletion permanent immediately.

How links stay useful

Automated checks produce a stale-resource report and separate confirmed dead URLs from sign-in, rate-limit and bot-blocked behavior. Manual review dates are kept separate from automated reachability.
